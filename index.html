The Craft

Three elements.
One living sculpture.

Photographs capture the shape. In person, the stones catch the light from every angle and the trees seem to glow.

i

The Stone

Every piece begins with a natural base: an amethyst geode, a hand-dug quartz cluster, a slab of pyrite, labradorite, celestite or blue slag glass. The stone sets the mood.

ii

The Tree

Trunk, roots and branches are twisted entirely by hand from fine wire, then shaped in the traditions of real bonsai: upright, windswept, cascading, willow.

iii

The Gems

Genuine gemstones become the foliage. Herkimer diamonds, garnet, ruby, sapphire, opal, peridot, aquamarine, freshwater pearls. Chosen to sparkle, placed one at a time.

Meet the Maker

Turning a love of bonsai
and sparkling stones into art

I have always felt most at peace in nature. Plants and trees bring me a sense of calm and joy, which led me to the beautiful art of bonsai. I fell in love with growing and shaping miniature trees, from tropical varieties to outdoor bonsai.

Wanting to share that joy in my own way, I combined it with my lifelong attraction to gemstones, sparkling crystals and beautiful natural rocks. That inspiration became Twisted Swann Studio.

Every tree has its own personality, movement and sparkle. Bring a little bougie home.
Jamie Swann-StojetzOwner · Designer · Maker
