The Making

Watch a tree
come to life

Black Opal & Herkimer Diamond · one of one

  1. Twist

    Dozens of strands of fine wire are gathered and twisted by hand into a trunk. No two ever turn the same way.

  2. Shape

    The strands split into roots and branches and are bent into a classic bonsai form: upright, windswept, cascading, willow.

  3. Adorn

    Genuine gemstones are wired on one at a time. This tree carries black opal, Herkimer diamond and smoky topaz.

  4. Rooted

    Set into a natural stone chosen for its character, here a sparkling sphalerite. Then it is ready for a home.

Meet the Maker

Turning a love of bonsai
and sparkling stones into art

I have always felt most at peace in nature. Plants and trees bring me a sense of calm and joy, which led me to the beautiful art of bonsai. I fell in love with growing and shaping miniature trees, from tropical varieties to outdoor bonsai.

Wanting to share that joy in my own way, I combined it with my lifelong attraction to gemstones, sparkling crystals and beautiful natural rocks. That inspiration became Twisted Swann Studio.

Every tree has its own personality, movement and sparkle. Bring a little bougie home.
Jamie Swann-StojetzOwner · Designer · Maker
